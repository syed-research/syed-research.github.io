# <span class="fade-up">Our Team</span>

The CLIMATE Research Group brings together researchers working across thermochemical engineering, CO₂ utilisation, chemical looping, and sustainable energy systems. Our work integrates experimental, computational, and analytical expertise to advance next‑generation decarbonisation technologies.

---
